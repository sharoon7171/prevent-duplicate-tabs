Prevent Duplicate Tabs is a Chrome extension that stops duplicate tabs before they clutter your browser. When you open a link that is already open, it detects the match and closes the extra tab—or switches you to the tab you already have—so you save memory, stay organized, and stop hunting through identical pages.

Install this duplicate tab blocker to automatically prevent duplicate tabs on every site, or only on the sites you choose. No account and no sign-in. Your settings and your local “duplicates prevented” count stay on your device.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHY PREVENT DUPLICATE TABS?
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Too many tabs slow Chrome down. Opening the same URL again wastes RAM, creates tab clutter, and makes it easy to lose your place. Whether you research, code, shop, or manage email, a focused tab manager that closes duplicate tabs keeps one useful copy of each page—unless you decide otherwise.

If you have ever asked how to stop Chrome from opening the same tab again, or how to close duplicate tabs automatically, this extension is built for that.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HOW IT WORKS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

When a new tab loads, Prevent Duplicate Tabs compares its URL with your open tabs—across all windows or only the current window. If it finds a duplicate, it runs your chosen action right away.

Set a global default, then override behavior per domain. For example, block duplicates on most sites while allowing multiple Gmail or Docs tabs where you need them. Choose prevention on all websites (with exceptions), or only on a monitored list of sites and pages.

When you turn the extension on or change scope or lists, it also scans tabs you already have open and cleans up duplicates that match your rules.

KEY FEATURES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Automatic duplicate detection
• Compares new tab URLs to open tabs—no manual cleanup.

Four actions when a duplicate appears
• Close the new duplicate and stay on your current tab.
• Close the old duplicate and stay where you are.
• Close the new tab and switch to the existing tab (and focus that window).
• Close the old tab and switch to the new one.

Matching and window scope
• Check duplicates in all windows, or only the current window.
• Optionally ignore URL parameters so pages that differ only by query strings (?id=1 vs ?id=2) count as the same page.

Prevention scope and site lists
• All websites—prevent duplicate tabs everywhere except domains or pages on your exceptions list.
• Listed sites only—prevent duplicates only on domains or pages you monitor; skip individual pages on a monitored domain when needed.
• One list on the options page for exceptions or monitored sites, with search, add, edit, and remove.
• Current-domain controls in the popup and options—toggle this page or this domain without typing URLs.

Per-site rules and quick controls
• Site-specific rules—set the duplicate action and “ignore URL parameters” per domain on the options page.
• Toolbar popup—enable or disable the extension, choose prevention scope, and adjust this page or this domain in one click.
• Options page—full control of scope, global defaults, site lists, and per-domain rules (right-click the extension icon → Options).

Open a deliberate duplicate
• Right-click a link and choose “Open in new tab (allow duplicate)” to open that URL without prevention when you intentionally want two copies.

Local stats
• See your open tab count and how many duplicates were prevented—stored only on your device.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PRIVACY AND PERMISSIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Uses tabs (read URLs, close or switch tabs, open from the context menu), storage (settings and local stats), windows (focus the correct window and close empty windows after cleanup), and contextMenus (“Open in new tab (allow duplicate)” on links). It does not use broad host permissions or content scripts on web pages. See the extension privacy policy for details.
